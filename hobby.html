Cars, Friends, and Good Times:
A CS Student's Life

Ervanthe Daniel Layskie · 📅 February 28, 2026 · ⏱ 5 min read

Being a Computer Science student doesn't mean I'm glued to my laptop 24/7. I love hanging out with friends, checking out cool cars online, and just enjoying life. Let me share what I'm all about beyond the code.

How I Got Into Cars

My interest in luxury cars came from my older brother (koko). He's always been into cars, watching YouTube reviews, scrolling through car Instagram accounts, and talking about the latest supercars. Growing up, I'd see him watching videos of Lamborghinis, Ferraris, and McLarens.

At first, I didn't really care. But the more I watched with him, the more I appreciated how beautiful these cars are. I'm not a car expert — I can't tell you about engine specs or 0-60 times. I just think they look amazing. Now it's become something my brother and I bond over.

The kind of cars my brother and I love looking at together

Life at Universitas Ciputra

Studying Computer Science is intense, but I try to keep a balance. Sure, I spend a lot of time coding, debugging, and working on projects. But I also make sure to have fun. My friends and I are always planning something — whether it's trying a new cafe, grabbing dinner somewhere, or just walking around the city.

Surabaya has so many cool spots to explore. We love finding new cafes with good vibes, or just hanging out at the mall. Sometimes we don't even do anything specific — just being together and talking is enough.

Why Hanging Out Matters

Some people might think hanging out is just "wasting time," but I disagree. Spending time with friends helps me relax and recharge. When I'm stressed about an assignment or stuck on a coding problem, going out and just chilling makes everything better.

We don't need to do anything fancy. Sometimes it's just sitting at a cafe, talking about random stuff — memes, movies, cars, or whatever's on our minds. Those simple moments are what I'll remember years from now.

💡 What I've Learned
  • Balance is Key: Study hard, but don't forget to have fun. Life is more than just academics.
  • Friendships Matter: Good friends make everything better, whether it's celebrating wins or getting through tough times.
  • Enjoy Simple Things: You don't need expensive hobbies. Looking at cool cars online or chilling at a cafe is enough.

Cars and Friends

Sometimes my friends think I'm weird for liking luxury cars so much. They'll tease me when I send them a Lamborghini post or when I get excited about a new car release. But they get it — everyone has their thing. Some are into gaming, some love sneakers, and I like looking at beautiful cars. It's just what I enjoy.

My brother and I still watch car videos together when I go home. It's our thing. He'll show me the latest Ferrari or McLaren reveal, and we'll talk about which one looks better. It's not deep — it's just fun. And that's what matters.

"Don't take life too seriously. Enjoy the little things, like cool cars and good friends."

— My life motto

Looking Ahead

I'm not sure what the future holds. Maybe I'll work as a software engineer, maybe I'll do something else entirely. For now, I'm just focused on finishing my degree, building cool projects, and making the most of my time with friends.

Will I ever own one of those luxury cars I love looking at? Who knows. But even if I don't, I'll always appreciate them. And I'll always value the time spent with my brother, my friends, and the simple moments that make life worth living. That's what matters most.
